# UYYM Academy

UYYM helps KBTU students prepare for Quiz, Midterm, Endterm and Final in small study groups. Initial subjects are Calculus I, Linear Algebra and PP1 (C++). The public site accepts course applications; the owner verifies applicants and manages accounts and access.

The private product has three roles. STUDENT uses enrolled courses, recordings, materials, homework and personal feedback. TEACHER manages assigned learning content and groups, with access management only when explicitly permitted. ADMIN manages applications, people, courses, assignments, enrollments, payments, browsers and settings.

The interface supports English, Kazakh and Russian, with English on a first visit. Light is the initial theme. Both preferences persist. Author-written course, lesson, timetable and feedback content stays in its original language. Display times use Asia/Almaty; stored dates remain UTC.

The React/Vite frontend uses a Fastify BFF. Supabase Auth/PostgreSQL and private Google Drive remain server-side integrations. Browser approval, cryptographic device proof, application sessions, enrollment checks and playback grants are binding constraints, including during every new media-part request.

Do not invent students, teachers, reviews, results, progress, payments, schedules or published video availability. Show only states supported by the current API. The workspace redesign adds no provider, paid feature, migration or production data change. Publication requires separate owner authorization.
